The quiet revenue gap

Scope creep rarely arrives as one big event.

It shows up as one extra revision, a “quick” implementation, or a setup task nobody remembered to add to the invoice. ScopeWallet is designed to help you notice those moments.

Why it matters

The work is done. The commercial conversation never happened.

When project activity and contract terms live in different places, it's easy for extra work to slip through unnoticed.

  • Work requests are scattered across tools and conversations.
  • Teams discover scope changes when it's too late to discuss them.
  • Invoices rely on what someone remembers to include.

A few clarifications

Good questions, straight answers.

ScopeWallet is being shaped with early users. Here's what to know about the pilot.

Does ScopeWallet automatically decide that work is out of scope?

No. A mismatch is a prompt for review. Your team should check the contract, project context and client agreement before deciding whether work is billable or requires a change order.

Is the product fully integrated with Slack, Jira, GitHub and email today?

The product vision includes comparing activity from project tools with contract scope. The pilot will validate the workflow and integration requirements; this page does not claim that every integration is already live.

What happens after I request pilot access?

Your details are submitted through the embedded Tally form. Once notifications are configured in Tally, the ScopeWallet team can follow up using the email address you provide.

Is the annual calculator a guarantee of savings?

No. It's a simple estimate based on the hours and rate you enter, multiplied by 48 working weeks. Actual missed billable work can be lower, higher, or zero.
